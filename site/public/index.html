What it is

What it is

A database is where your app keeps everything it must not forget.

Names, prices, bookings, messages, who paid and who did not. A spreadsheet holds that until two people open it at once, or it reaches ten thousand rows, or the laptop it lives on is lost. A database holds it for as many people as you like, at the same time, from anywhere — and it is what every real app sits on top of.

Normally you would rent a server, install the software, set up backups, watch it for trouble and pay every month whether anyone used it or not. Barua does all of that part. You ask for a database, you get an address and a password, and you give those to whoever is building your app.

What you get

Everything the boring part needs, already done.

Ready in seconds

Click once. You get an address and a password, and it is live. No server to rent, nothing to install.

A spare copy, always

Your data is copied off the machine continuously. If something is deleted by mistake, you can go back to how things were on Tuesday at 09:14.

Look at your data yourself

Open it in your browser and read the rows like a spreadsheet. Change a wrong phone number, search for an order, without asking a developer.

It stays yours

One button downloads the whole thing as a file you can keep or take elsewhere. Nothing locks you in.

It rests when you do

After five quiet minutes it stops, and wakes the moment someone visits. That is why a quiet month costs nothing.

A copy to practise on

Make a duplicate of today's data in a moment, try something risky on it, and leave the real one untouched.
